About

I started as a respiratory therapist at a 1,300-bed hospital in Taiwan. During the COVID-19 supply shortage I stopped waiting for a solution to arrive and led one instead — an internal innovation project that reached 82% adoption across the hospital and won the 15th National Innovation Award.

That taught me the thing I have built a career on: a product succeeds when the person holding it changes what they do next. Everything after — go-to-market at Baxter, product strategy at a London startup, an MSc in International Marketing at King's — has been about making that happen on purpose, at scale, with the numbers to show for it.

I work best in the gap between the clinical detail and the commercial decision, where most of the translation gets lost.
